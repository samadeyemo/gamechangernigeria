# Game Changers Nigeria 🇳🇬

### Learn. Connect. Lead. Impact.

Game Changers Nigeria is a leadership development movement committed to building ethical, competent, and purpose-driven leaders who create meaningful change in their communities and contribute to Nigeria's development.

The platform connects people with leadership education, mentorship, community chapters, development opportunities, and practical pathways to turn ideas into action.

**Website:** https://gamechangersnigeria.org/

## 🎯 Our Mission

To develop people who are ready to lead by providing practical learning experiences, meaningful relationships, opportunities to serve, and platforms for responsible leadership.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Leadership Academy:** Access leadership development programmes, learning resources, and educational opportunities.
- **Community Network:** Discover members, connect with local chapters, and build relationships with like-minded people.
- **Leadership Development:** Explore opportunities to strengthen leadership capacity, character, and responsible citizenship.
- **Mentorship:** Connect with experienced individuals who can support personal and professional growth.
- **Impact Initiatives:** Highlight projects and activities designed to create measurable community impact.
- **Opportunities:** Discover ways to participate, contribute, volunteer, and grow within the movement.
- **Chapter Management:** Support the establishment and development of local Game Changers communities.
- **Member Hub:** Provide members with a central place to access relevant platform resources and member services.
- **Stories and Resources:** Share leadership insights, community stories, educational materials, and ideas for positive change.
- **Partnerships:** Create opportunities for individuals and organisations to collaborate with the movement.

## 🧭 Our Core Principles

Game Changers Nigeria is guided by the following values:

- Integrity
- Excellence
- Responsibility
- Service
- Innovation
- Respect
- Accountability

The movement encourages people to become solution providers, practise servant leadership, and translate knowledge into responsible action.

### Installation

1. Clone the repository:


2. Navigate to the project directory:
